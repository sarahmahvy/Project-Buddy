Problem Analysis
The Dilemma: Why Great Student Ideas Die Alone
Fear of Idea Theft
  • Students hesitate to share creative blueprints on open groups or Discord servers.
  • Posting full project architectures risks premature copying by peers before launch.
  • Result: Students work in isolation, miss hackathons, or abandon ideas.
Skill Mismatch
  • Modern projects require multi-disciplinary skills: AI + IoT + Design + Cloud.
  • Students rarely know peers outside their section or department.
  • No objective compatibility scoring to evaluate prospective teammates.
Surveillance & Bias
  • Campus forums expose student ideas to staff grading bias and surveillance.
  • Unmoderated channels suffer from academic spam, phishing, and ghosting.
  • Lack of privacy-preserving governance to protect student IP.
Value Proposition
The Solution: Safe & Verified Collaboration
What Makes ProjectBuddy Unique
  • Anti-Theft Idea Vault: Only the project title and tech stack are public. Full blueprints remain 100% encrypted.
  • Mutual Lock & Key: Detailed architecture is unlocked ONLY when the creator accepts a partnership proposal.
  • Skill Compatibility Engine: Real-time match percentage calculation (e.g. 92% Match).
  • Anonymous Admin Audit: Institutional oversight with zero student surveillance.
Measurable Academic Impact
  • 10x Increase in Idea Sharing: Students post freely knowing their IP cannot be copied.
  • Interdisciplinary Pairing: Seamless cross-pollination between CS, EE, Data Science, and Design.
  • Reduced Formation Time: Partner search cycles cut from weeks down to hours.
  • Higher Project Completion: Mutual collaboration commitments keep teams accountable.
Core Architecture
Feature 1: The Anti-Theft Idea Vault
1. Public Discovery State
  • Title: Visible across directory (e.g. "AI Study Planner")
  • Domain: Categorized by academic department
  • Difficulty: Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced badge
  • Required Skills: Tech stack tags for filtering
  • ENCRYPTED BLUEPRINT: Detailed implementation, architecture diagrams, and methodology are shielded behind a lock box.
2. Unlocked Partnership State
  • Trigger: Creator reviews proposal and clicks "Accept".
  • Access: Full project description unlocks for the partner.
  • Collaboration Room: Dedicated workspace with live messaging and milestone progress tracking.
  • Digital NDA: Mutual trust agreement activated automatically.
Governance & Privacy
Feature 2: Zero-Knowledge Administrative Audit
Zero-Knowledge Administrative Monitoring Protocol All personal identifiers (legal full names, personal phone numbers, institutional email addresses, and unreleased private blueprints) are strictly scrubbed. Administrators monitor platform safety purely via pseudonymized tokens.
Anonymous Telemetry
  • Displays tokens like STU_#849201.
  • Tracks activity by department and year.
  • Allows freezing malicious actors without uncovering their personal identity.
Sitewide Broadcasts
  • Push real-time alert banners to all student screens.
  • Ideal for hackathon announcements, deadlines, and security reminders.
Title Moderation
  • Audit published project titles for community standards.
  • 1-click takedown of spam or abusive entries while private drafts remain hidden.

Security Framework
Security, Privacy & IP Safeguards
Student IP Safeguards
  • No Scraping: REST API sanitizes description fields unless request is from creator or accepted partner.
  • Timestamp Priority: Cryptographic timestamps prove concept origination date.
  • Mutual Commitment: Digital agreement guarantees fair project attribution.
  • Reporting Desk: Students can flag duplicate or plagiarized project titles.
Zero-Knowledge Admin Safeguards
  • Zero PII Exposure: Real names, emails, and phone numbers are absent from admin views.
  • Pseudonymized Auditing: Accounts are inspected as anonymous tokens (e.g. STU_#4820).
  • Shielded Blueprints: Administrators cannot view private idea texts, eliminating institutional bias.
  • Safe Intervention: Spam or malicious accounts are frozen without invading student privacy.
